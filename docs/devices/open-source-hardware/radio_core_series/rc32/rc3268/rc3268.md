The RC3268 is a compact IoT development board featuring the ESP32-S3R8 MCU and HC01 Wi-Fi HaLow Module. It combines Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0/BLE, and Wi-Fi HaLow connectivity, with support for both AP mode and Client mode to meet different application requirements. Compatible with Arduino, PlatformIO, and ESP-IDF, it enables rapid development of long-range IoT solutions.


## Product Characteristics

- Features the ESP32-S3R8 MCU and HC01 Wi-Fi HaLow module, supporting Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0/BLE, and HaLow connectivity.
- Supports HaLow AP and Client modes.
- Compatible with Arduino, ESP-IDF, and PlatformIO.
- Compact solder-free design for easy prototyping.
